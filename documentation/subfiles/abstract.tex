    \begin{abstract}
        In this project, we brought three navigation functionalities from the course Autonomous Mobile Robots to the Robile platform: path and motion planning with A* and a potential field planner, Monte Carlo localisation with our own particle filter, and fringe-based exploration together with gmapping for SLAM.
        All functionalities work reliably in the simulation: the robot reaches its goals, localises itself globally in a known map and explores an unknown environment completely.
        On the real robot, we had to solve several additional problems, such as different clock times of the laptop and the robot, invalid laser measurements and collisions with the corners of the robot.
        A narrow passage in our test arena is still difficult for the robot, if it enters the passage at an angle.
    \end{abstract}
